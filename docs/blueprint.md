# **App Name**: Grupo Empresarial

## Core Features:

- Company Overview: Provide a general overview of Grupo Empresarial, showcasing its mission, values, and history.
- Department Showcase: Highlight the Development and Call Center departments, detailing their roles, responsibilities, and key personnel (5 developers, 8 call center staff).
- Employee Login: Implement a secure login and authentication system for employees to access the intranet.
- Intranet Access: Allow authenticated employees to view intranet information, including announcements, payroll details, and a company directory. It is necessary to integrate with Firestore.
- Document storage: Securely store documents in cloud storage.
- Smart Content Generation: Use AI to generate content, based on data stored in Firestore, such as department performance summaries.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep ocean blue (#2D3E50), inspired by the company logo's darker blue shade. This represents intelligence and stability.
- Background color: Light, desaturated blue-gray (#E8EBF0). It is similar in hue to the primary, but soft and neutral for comfortable reading.
- Accent color: Vibrant turquoise (#30D5C8), drawing from the logo's lighter teal, to highlight interactive elements and calls to action.
- Body text: 'Inter', a sans-serif font providing a modern and objective look, ensuring readability for announcements, payroll, and directory information.
- Headline text: 'Space Grotesk', a proportional sans-serif font providing a computerized and scientific look that gives it a techy look.
- Use clean, modern icons representing different sections of the intranet and company information.
- Employ a grid-based layout for clear information hierarchy, separating public-facing content from employee intranet sections.
